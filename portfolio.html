America250 Mindelo — Educational Workshops

Period
March–July 2026
Role
Workshop Developer, Organizer & Facilitator
Location
Mindelo, Cabo Verde
Context and purpose
I developed the Independence, Opportunity & the Future workshop sequence in Mindelo as part of the America250 educational context. The overarching purpose was to help participants look beyond perceived limitations, imagine a meaningful future, discover resources that could make progress possible, and identify realistic first steps. The program aimed to build agency and confidence through direction, practical tools and concrete examples of accessible opportunities; it does not claim measured long-term outcomes.
Three connected workshops
  1. June 11 — What Do I Want to Build? / O Que Quero Construir? I guided participants through strategic futuring, future visioning and backcasting: considering what they wanted to build in their lives, identifying a possible direction, then working backward from that vision to skills, relationships, resources and an achievable next step. The emphasis was on expanding perceived possibilities without ignoring real circumstances.
  2. June 18 — Find Your Next Step / Encontra o Teu Próximo Passo Building directly on the first session, I helped participants connect their emerging goals to practical tools. We explored free educational and career-development resources, digital tools and online learning opportunities, using the independent Learning & Career Resource Hub as a supporting resource. The intention was to help people recognize that financial or educational barriers do not always mean they have no way to begin; accessible resources can support an informed, tangible first step.
  3. June 23 — Three Pathways to Opportunity The third workshop provided concrete examples of available online training through the OPEN/MOOC learning pathways then being presented: English for Tourism and Hospitality, English for Career Development, and English for Business and Entrepreneurship. Participants were introduced to the course options, online-learning format and ways to begin. These were examples of possible paths, not promises of enrollment, credentials or employment.
My role
I independently conceived and developed the workshop structure, presentation materials, participant resources, and promotional graphics, directing AI-assisted production under my supervision. I coordinated scheduling and logistics with American Spaces representatives and local educational stakeholders, personally conducted the workshops and facilitated discussion with participants.
Supporting artifacts
I created the Workshop 1 participant recap, What Do I Want to Build? / O Que Quero Construir?, and developed related program materials. Original workshop photographs, flyers, presentations and recaps document the delivered educational sequence.
Professional significance
This work connected program design, direct facilitation and practical digital tools. It also demonstrates how the independently built Resource Hub was used as a supporting instrument for real-world educational outreach.
Historical scope
This work reflects historical educational collaboration in Mindelo from March–July 2026, not a formal institutional appointment or current organizational partnership.

A PRASA — Independent Digital Public Square

Period
July 2026–Present
Role
Founder & Principal Consultant
Context
Useful local information is often fragmented: opportunities, organizations, practical resources, culture, local activities, and ways to contribute may appear on different platforms and sources.
My work
I founded A PRASA in July 2026 to develop a broader, independent, source-led digital public square for Cabo Verde. I set the initiative's direction and independently directed AI-assisted research, content development, website implementation, review, and continued iteration.
Public platform
The site presents selected information and pathways toward original sources and providers. Its public editorial function is separate from the company's commercial professional-services activity. Listing or linking to an organization is not itself verification, accreditation, sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation.
Development relationship
Experience gained through building the independent Resource Hub helped inform A PRASA's approach to discoverability, structured information, user navigation, and practical community access.
Status
Public operating initiative, continuing to evolve. Future features, geographic expansion, impact metrics, or institutional partnerships are not presented as completed or endorsed.
