Founding Preview

A PRASA is a shared place where paths, people, and possibilities meet.

A PRASA is an independent platform being built to make useful opportunities and practical local information easier to find and reach.

Think of it as a small digital praça: a place to discover useful starting points and go directly to the people and organizations behind them.

This early edition begins with a carefully limited selection rather than trying to cover everything at once.

A PRASA will grow gradually as information is reviewed, improved and shown to be genuinely useful.

HP Foundation / HP LIFE

HP LIFE: free business and digital-skills courses

HP LIFE offers 32 free online courses in business and digital skills. HP LIFE states that all 32 courses are available in eight languages, including Portuguese. Users must create an account and be logged in to enroll.

After completing a course, learners can receive a free Certificate of Completion. The certificate is not accredited or endorsed by educational bodies as an academic or professional qualification.

Checked 8 August 2026 against information published by the provider.

The Open University / OpenLearn

OpenLearn: free courses from The Open University

OpenLearn offers hundreds of free courses across nine subject areas, including computing, languages, business, science and society. The provider states that courses can be started immediately.

The reviewed interface and content were in English; Portuguese availability was not confirmed. A free account may be needed to track progress and access some participation or achievement records. Not every course offers formal credit, certification or digital badges.

Checked 8 August 2026 against information published by the provider.

Instituto do Emprego e Formação Profissional (IEFP)

IEFP PEPE: employment and professional-internship portal

PEPE is the IEFP platform for employment and professional-internship opportunities. Visitors can view opportunities, create a candidate account and sign in to apply through the provider.

An account and sign-in are required to apply. Each listing has its own conditions and deadline. Opportunities change frequently, so confirm current availability on the platform.
